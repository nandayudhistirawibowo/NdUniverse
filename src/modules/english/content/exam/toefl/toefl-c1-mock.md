    Researchers mitigate this by employing error‑correction codes and by cooling the system to near absolute
    zero using dilution refrigerators. While we’re still in the noisy intermediate‑scale quantum (NISQ)
    era, recent experiments have demonstrated quantum supremacy, meaning a quantum device performed a
    specific task faster than the world’s most powerful supercomputer. The implications are vast, ranging
    from cryptography to drug discovery, but practical, large‑scale quantum computers may still be a decade
    or more away.'
